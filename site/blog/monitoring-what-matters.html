RELIABILITY / INSIGHTS

Monitor what actually matters

Green lights on a dashboard mean little if customers still cannot finish their work.

Start with the customer journey

Identify business-critical actions such as signing in, submitting an order, or creating an invoice. Monitor whether those journeys succeed.

Make alerts actionable

Every alert should tell someone what failed, why it matters, and what to check next. Too many low-value alerts train teams to ignore everything.

Practice the recovery

Test backups, escalation paths, and incident communication. Monitoring detects a problem; preparedness determines how well you recover.

Improve the signal

Review incidents and false alarms periodically. Keep measurements tied to outcomes, not collecting metrics for their own sake.
