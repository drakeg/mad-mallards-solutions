AUTOMATION / INSIGHTS

Automation without the hype

The best automation projects start with a clear business problem, not a shiny tool.

Find the repetitive work

Look for tasks people repeat every day or week. Document each handoff, copy-and-paste, approval and recurring source of mistakes. If the work is unpredictable or poorly understood, automate later.

Design the smallest useful change

Choose one workflow with a clear owner and measurable outcome. Make sure people can review the result, understand failures, and intervene when something unexpected happens.

Keep people where they add value

Automation should remove unnecessary effort, not accountability. Preserve review for sensitive decisions and start with a pilot before expanding.

What to measure

Track time spent, rework, errors, and completion time before and after. Improvements should be demonstrated, not assumed.
