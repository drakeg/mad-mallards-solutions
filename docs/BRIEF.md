# Mad Mallards Solutions — Scroll Craft creative brief

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** User supplied name, audience, style, scroll structure, asset choice, and key visitor actions. The remaining design decisions below are authored, not quotations from the user.

## Confirmed requirements
- Brand: Mad Mallards Solutions, new site.
- Audience: small business owners and IT consulting prospects.
- Visual direction: minimal.
- Journey: distinct scroll scenes.
- Assets: mixed.
- Desired outcomes: Subscribe, Contact, read Blog.
- Work: GitHub repository, local preview, installed Scroll Craft skill, Mad Mallard Development Standards.
- Creative authority: "Use your creative judgment."

## Authored creative decisions
- Vibe: calm, clear, competent, quietly optimistic, editorial.
- References: modern independent design studio editorial sites and technical print journals (no existing site cloned).
- Journey: recognition of unnecessary complexity → relief from friction → practical service options → interactive before/after comparison → understandable implementation process → journal for deeper reading → contact form and newsletter interest.
- Energy: spacious opening, concentrated dark statement, rational service exploration, engaging interactive comparison, steady process, reflective journal, useful close.
- Feeling curve: recognition (hero shows a simple promise) → confidence (dark statement cuts clutter) → clarity (services) → agency (challenge selector) → reassurance (transparent steps) → curiosity (journal) → readiness (usable form).
- One peak: the visitor switches a challenge and sees messy work reframe as an actionable, clearer system. "It's the site where the pain turns into the plan."
- Signature move: three-state before/after business friction explorer, functional without requiring scroll.
- Aesthetic family: premium minimal, muted green and warm paper, tactile geometric self-authored brand symbol.
- Structure: chaptered scenes. Editorial presentation using flow and a restrained high-contrast statement, as the user wanted scenes rather than a continuous cinematic film.
- Assets: original SVG and CSS geometry; no paid imagery, unlicensed stock or fabricated testimonials.
- CTA: contact as main commercial action; blog and subscribe as requested secondary paths.
- Authored quiet: journal and subscribe sections are intentionally reduced in motion.

## Scroll score
| Beat | Device family | Intended feeling |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | layered hero / restrained parallax | immediate calm |
| Belief | editorial hard-cut statement + entrance reveal | conviction |
| Range | flow + entrance reveals | possibility |
| Transformation | functional interactive comparison | agency (peak) |
| Method | high-contrast chapter transition + scroll reveals | reassurance |
| Journal | editorial navigation | curiosity |
| Commitment | form and subscription input | readiness |

**Uniqueness:** initial local fingerprint registry is empty. Compared with existing Scroll Craft examples, this site uses an editorial service narrative, geometric identity, interactive process comparison, understated navigation and a dual contact/subscription conclusion; this is not a cinematic hero scrub, pan-rail product catalog, or magnetic close.

**Limitations:** Headless Chrome does not establish actual iOS or Android device behavior. Newsletter sending and outbound contact email are not implemented; the SQLite database persists local submissions.
