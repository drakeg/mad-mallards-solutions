CLOUD / INSIGHTS

Does your business need the cloud?

The right answer is not always move everything. Start with what your business needs to run reliably.

Understand the problem first

A server that works today does not need replacing solely because it is not in a cloud platform. Consider reliability, security, staff expertise, cost, and growth.

Look at the full cost

Hosting is only one part of the cost. Include backups, licenses, networking, support, monitoring, migration, and the people needed to operate a service.

Design for recovery

Know how your data is backed up, how long restoration takes, and who makes decisions during an outage. A clear recovery plan is valuable wherever workloads run.

Choose a proportionate path

A hybrid approach or managed service may solve the immediate problem without wholesale migration. Move in stages, test and keep an exit plan.
