Privacy

Last updated November 2026. This prototype stores the name, email address, and message you submit through the contact form, and email addresses submitted to subscribe. Records are stored in a server-side SQLite database for follow-up. If the operator enables SMTP notifications, contact messages are also forwarded to the configured email provider and inbox. Newsletter addresses remain pending; outbound newsletter sending is not active. We do not collect payment details.

Submitted information is used for inquiries and subscriptions. We do not sell subscriber information. Database access, backups and retention policies must be secured before deployment to the public internet.

Request correction or removal using the contact form. This preliminary notice requires review for the actual hosting, jurisdiction, email provider, and operating practices before production launch.
